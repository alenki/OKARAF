The idea

Your card suits become a tailor’s suits. Websites, automations and AI are cut to measure, and the site is the atelier. The dither becomes the weave of the cloth.

What you play with

A pinstripe cloth you can grab. A label that stitches in the visitor’s name (?for=Stripe on job applications). A tape measure that tracks scroll. A swatch book that re-weaves the whole site.

Why it is yours

“Class”, “look intelligent” and the suits were already on your site. This takes them literally. The old values marquee returns as the selvedge woven along the cloth’s edge.

Watch out

The metaphor has to stay light: headings and details only, plain language everywhere else, or it turns into costume.

The idea

The portfolio is a deck of engraved 1-bit cards. “Full stack” becomes “full deck”. Every project, service and fact is a card you hold.

What you play with

Real card physics: drag, flip, throw and shuffle. Spades are your sites, clubs your automations, diamonds your AI. The king is you.

Why it is yours

Suits and the king come straight from your old marquee. It is the most direct reading of them, and the most toy-like of the three.

Watch out

Playing-card portfolios are a known genre, and the mood drifts toward games and gambling, which may sit oddly beside “faith” in your values.

The idea

Your motto as a rendering rule. Dithering makes two colours look like a million: it is the art of looking intelligent. The whole site obeys it.

What you play with

A lens that resolves 1-bit into full detail. A threshold dial. A dither lab that turns any photo into a 1-bit portrait card to keep.

Why it is yours

The closest to the current site: a direct evolution of the dithered hero, pushed until it is the entire identity.

Watch out

Dither and retro-computing are a visible trend right now. With no story on top, it has to win on craft alone.

True in all three

The site is black and white, so the work is the only colour on it.
