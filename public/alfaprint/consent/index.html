Consent to the processing of personal data

This page says which of your data we record, why and for how long. No legalese: if anything is unclear, call us and ask in plain words.

  1. 01

    Who records it

    Alfa advertising agency, Nalchik, 29 Tarchokova St. The same workshop your order goes to, and the same phone you call about anything else: +7 967 425-79-79.

  2. 02

    What exactly

    Only what you typed into the form yourself: your name, how to reach you and the text of your message. If you came from the order page, the contents of the run are added: what we print, how many and on what.

    The site knows nothing else about you and has no way to find out.

  3. 03

    Why

    To reply to you, price your order and print it. For nothing else: we send no newsletters, and we never pass on or sell your data.

    What we do with it: record it, store it, read it and reply. Nothing automated happens to it, and no machine makes decisions about you.

  4. 04

    How long we keep it

    For as long as the conversation and the order last. After that your message stays in the order history: a repeat run is printed from the layout already approved, so it doesn’t need pricing again.

    Ask us to erase it and we will. The order history goes along with your message.

  5. 05

    How to withdraw

    Call +7 967 425-79-79 or write to the same WhatsApp and say it in plain words: delete my data. There is no separate withdrawal form; a single sentence doesn’t need one.

    We’ll erase it and tell you it’s done. The same way, you can ask to see what we have on record about you, or to correct anything wrong.

  6. 06

    What the site itself does

    It doesn’t track you. There is no Yandex Metrica, no Google Analytics, no third-party counters or pixels on any page, and the site sets no cookies that identify a person.

    A sent form reaches us at once, as a single message, with exactly the text shown on the sheet to the right of the form. It has no hidden fields.
