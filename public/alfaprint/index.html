From idea to finished piece: printing, cutting, engraving and finishing all happen at 29 Tarchokova St. We choose the material and print method to fit the job, the deadline and the budget. More than 48,000 items pass through the workshop every month.

The price depends on the item, the quantity, the material and the deadline. You can see the ballpark before you call; we name the exact figure once we see the layout.

Paper is printed in color and trimmed to size. Runs here are counted in hundreds, not thousands.

Print for indoor spaces, where people look at it up close: a fine dot and inks with almost no smell.

Print that is read from across the street. The dot is coarser, but the sheet comes in one piece.

Ultraviolet light cures the ink right on the object: it sets on hard surfaces without soaking in. On fabric, the same image goes on by transfer.

Printing from a plate, which only pays off on a long run. Make-ready takes a while, then the press runs fast.

The beam goes right through the material. Here the image is the outline itself and the light that passes through it.

The top layer comes off and the mark goes deep: it does not sit on the object, it is taken out of it.

The blade cuts the film along the outline without touching the backing. The color here comes from the film itself.

The design is laid down in thread, straight onto the fabric. Nothing can wash off: the thread is the image.

The logo does not sit on top, it goes into the object: cut into metal, cast in chocolate.
